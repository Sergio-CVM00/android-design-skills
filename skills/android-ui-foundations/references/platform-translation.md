# Translating a design to Android

Keep the user goal, content, and brand; map the behavior to Android conventions.

Begin with a flow map and separate parent sections, children, and modal tasks. Work on a copy of the source design when editing design files.

Replace platform chrome and evaluate a range of window sizes. The guide's 412 dp starting frame and 360 dp example are design samples, not a supported-device boundary. A single resized artboard does not establish adaptation.

Map primary tab navigation to appropriate Android navigation. Review secondary items such as profile/settings before crowding the primary navigation. Map top navigation to app bars and choose Up, close, or no leading navigation icon according to hierarchy.

Translate modality by purpose. A source-platform sheet can become an Android bottom sheet or a full-screen task; do not mechanically turn every sheet into the same component. Distinguish dismissal from saving.

Use Android input and selection conventions. Check tabs for sibling content, native sharing where appropriate, and list grouping without unnecessary disclosure indicators or dividers.

Retain an existing shared design system when it remains usable on Android. Adjust color roles, scalable type, icon style, shape, and motion as needed. Do not mandate Roboto or a purple Material baseline over the user's brand.

Reconnect the prototype and exercise the complete flow, especially Back versus Up, system bars, keyboard, and cancellation. Review more than the visual asset swap.

Source: [Translate iOS designs to Android](https://developer.android.com/design/ui/mobile/guides/foundations/translate-designs), reviewed 2026-10-04.
